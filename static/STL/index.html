00 // PROJECT NOTE

Community-facing note, not a timeline event

The first text block addresses readers and explains the purpose of the work. It is shown here as editorial context, not as a historical community event.

Thank you very much for reading this timeline.

Dance, as an art form, suffers from its volatile nature.

Scholars all over the world are trying to mitigate flaws in recent decades.

To us, Shufflers, being both a rave and a social media culture creates unique challenges that most people do not care about.

This timeline is a collaborative work built to provide a historically accurate, informative, digestible and trustworthy source about Shuffling history, its years and constant changes.

It has required years of research and verification so new Shufflers and enthusiasts can learn without major doubts.

Remember: it is an endless work in progress.

If you have information to add or change, contact the project so it can be updated.

KEEP SHUFFLING AND LEARNING.

Kind regards, Gaara (Australia) and LuVa (Brazil) · @shuffletimeline
