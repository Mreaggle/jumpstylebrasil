## 1987–1990 — Aciiiiiied !!!

Era: Underground Era
Confidence: clear

- Foundations of the raves and Shuffling, as well as the first rave dance style known today as UK Rave Stepping.
- Dropfoot was the local terminology; Foot Patrol are identified as pioneers.
- The Second Summer of Love and Manchester are identified as cultural breakthroughs.
- Acid House, Breakbeat and Jungle are listed as musical context.
- Leeroy Thornhill, from The Prodigy, is named as a major influence on Melbourne ravers.
- Running Man appears as a ghetto dance fad, with Janet Jackson, MC Hammer and Vanilla Ice named in the image.

## 1989–1992 — Birth of Melbourne rave scene

Era: Underground Era
Confidence: clear

- The dance was called Stomping and is known today as Pre-Melbourne Shuffle.
- The image links this movement to heavy influence from British dance styles, such as Northern Soul.
- Raves at the Docks warehouse — Shed 14 — are listed.
- The Melbourne scene was inspired/noticed by the UK rave scene.

Links:
- [Melbourne Shuffle overview](https://knowyourmeme.com/sensitive/memes/melbourne-shuffle)

## 1993–1997 — The scene at its peak

Era: Underground Era
Confidence: supported

- Melbourne Underground Development (MUD) and Television Unlimited (TTU) organized raves and illegal broadcasts.
- Gary Shepherd is cited as witnessing the birth of the Melbourne Shuffle and sharing testimony on his blog.
- First online discussions appear in forums including inthemix.com.au, melbourneshuffle.com and clept.net.
- Exhibitions at clubs and appearance in The Age are referenced.
- Phat pants and hoodies become the standard Melbourne raver outfit.
- Street raves become common.

## 1998–2003 — (Oldschool or Original) Melbourne Shuffle

Era: Transition Era
Confidence: supported

- The name Melbourne Shuffle begins being used as the official name of the dance.
- Rupert Keiller, member of Sonic Animation, mentions the name in national television around 2000.
- T-Shuffle becomes the main move.
- Shift toward harder EDM styles and growth of Melbourne hard dance clubs including PHD and Hard Kandy.
- The Docks are shut down.
- Rave drug culture increases, especially GHB and meth.
- Phat pants design is consolidated by Chris Barry around 2000 via phatpants.com.
- Rocking as a style/term forms.
- Phat pants and hoodies become more visible.
- First videos are uploaded on YouTube.

## 2006–2011 — From Underground to Underage, then Worldwide

Era: YouTube & Facebook Era
Confidence: supported

- Malaysian students in Melbourne learn to Shuffle and spread the culture in Malaysia.
- The Malaysian scene reaches a peak, with local competitions and group choreographies.
- XES.CX is named as one of the oldest sources.
- Hardstyle Republic is formed.
- Hardstyle Shuffle trends across Australia and Malaysia.
- Melbshuffle.com forum is created around 2007.
- Jack40's Compilation 3 is framed as a cultural breakthrough on YouTube.
- Underage Shuffle meetups in public spaces, such as Federation Square, are listed.
- Pae & Sarah's famous duo video, used by Viktor in League of Legends, is referenced.
- Generation conflicts between Melbourne ravers and 2001 shufflers are noted.
- AUS and MAS terms are created to differentiate local differences.
- Local and online crew culture starts.
- Many consider this era as the only Melbourne Shuffle in existence.

## 2011–2014 — Cutting Shapes

Era: YouTube & Facebook Era
Confidence: supported

- Cutting Shapes starts to be used as a term referring to movements with the whole body in the UK.
- However, the image notes that Shuffling was referencing footwork only.
- It was mostly danced by Black people.
- #IGOTSHAPES YouTube series is referenced.
- Also known as Shapeshifting, UK Shuffle, House Shuffle or London Shuffle.
- In 2013 the dance was banned at some clubs, starting the Shuffle Wars.
- Release documentary in 2013, with testimonies and footage of the scene.
- Original Cutting Shapes scene from the UK fades away.
- Fortnite uses @gabbyjdavid's choreography, named Electro Shuffle, without authorization.
- Unknown to Melbourne Shufflers until 2019.

## 2015–2016 — Instagram transition

Era: Instagram & TikTok Era
Confidence: clear

- Instagram allowed 15-second videos in August 2015.
- Shuffling trends on Instagram in late 2015.
- Melbourne's White Night showcase is described as one of the last remaining pockets in its homeland.
- LED sneakers are used by many shufflers until 2019.

## 2015–2019 — New foundations

Era: Instagram & TikTok Era
Confidence: supported

- Massive growth in female presence, headed by North Americans.
- Rise of Shuffle influencers, sponsorships and live performances at festivals.
- Shuffle performers mixing Go-Go Dancing and Shuffle in the USA.
- Debates and critiques regarding female clothing and viewership.
- Russian, Spanish and Latin American scenes grow.
- Ghost Step Dance is practiced by thousands in China, including public parks.
- Phat pants make a comeback among underage dancers on TikTok.
- The terms Cutting Shapes and Shuffle begin being used as synonyms without clear distinctions.
- Hybrid Shuffle appears as an alternate term.

## 2019–2024 — Pilgrimage and Professionalization

Era: Instagram & TikTok Era
Confidence: clear

- Shufflers travel to teach and compete in many countries.
- Work on Shuffle Timeline begins in mid-2020.
- Shufflolution appears on America's Got Talent.
- Shuffle performers mix Shuffling with Go-Go Dancing in the USA.
- Hardstyle Shuffle and Jumpstyle trend on TikTok.
- Hardstyle Republic performance at Hakken Fantasia 2021.
- Melbourne Shufflers videoclip from The Netherlands is referenced.
- The trend dies in Australia, becoming a joke.
- Online shuffle tournaments increase globally.
- New styles emerge in other countries.
- Crews online run tryouts for new members.
- Overedited videos and online debates are listed.
- Shuffle classes are taught in dance studios across the USA and Europe, and online during the Covid-19 pandemic through monthly subscriptions.
- Resurgence of the Malaysian scene and Hardstyle Republic.
- Vanesa Seco's interview at KTLA Morning News.
- Flow Complex's last event is listed as March 2024.
